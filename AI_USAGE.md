# AI Usage Documentation

## 1. Overview

AI Tools were used throughout the process of creating the Pursuit Job Application Tracker to aid in Visual Design, technical challenges, and understanding the technologies that were being used. This application required me to connect the Flask application with a MySQL database, so there were times where additional guidance was needed especially when it came to troubleshooting errors. 

The primary AI tool used was ChatGPT to help explain concepts to me that I was a bit unsure about. Even then AI was used as a tool for additional knowledge rather then being a supplementation for critical thinking. 

## How AI was used During Development

### MySQL and Database Design

The first area which needed additional AI help was while i was creating the database structure. I wanted to ensure that all of the information such as contacts, companies, job listings and applications were relating without being duplicated. I used ChatGPT to understand the structure of the tables and the SQL statements. However, after I understood the structure, I examined the records, ran the scripts, and checked if everything was running as expected. AI was a great tool that helped me further understand technical SQL statements that I was unsure about. 

AI was also very helpful when it came to debugging. For example, there was instances when i would encounter issues with the records of the database returning NULL. I would share the error with AI and used the explanation it gave to understand the situation in a deeper manner.

Lastly, AI was used to help improve the appearance of the website while still keeping the original color palette of beige and forest green colors. Such aid was used during the process of refining the application page and the dashboard.

## My Approach to AI-Generated Content

Although AI was a great tool to help me during the process of creating the Pursuit application, it was still imperative to me that I made adjustments based on the requirements of the project. I checked to make sure all of the database queries were working and made sure the functionality was not changed during design change. When something would cause an error, I would use that error to troubleshoot instead of just blindly copying the code. 

## Reflection

Using AI tools have allowed me to become more comfortable with used MySQL and Flask. Throughout the process I have learned that getting the code generated is just one part of the process. Testing it, understanding it, and fixing the challenges are equally as important. AI has overall allowed me to work more efficiently while still acting as an aid to learn throughout the development of the Pursuit application.
